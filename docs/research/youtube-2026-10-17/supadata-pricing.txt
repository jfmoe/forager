## Credit usage [Permalink for this section](https://supadata.ai/pricing\#credit-usage)

Fetching a website URL or getting a transcript is 1 credit. If a video does not have a transcript yet, you can choose to generate it with AI.

- 1 transcript = 1 credit
- 1 generated transcript minute = 2 credits
- 1 batch video = 1 credit
- 1 video, channel or playlist = 1 credit
- 1 list of channel or playlist videos = 1 credit
- 1 search page (20 results) = 1 credit
- 1 minute of transcript translation = 30 credits
- 1 extraction minute = 5 credits (min 5)
- 1 URL = 1 credit
- 1 site map = 1 credit

## Auto Recharge [Permalink for this section](https://supadata.ai/pricing\#auto-recharge)

Request limits are applied based on your subscription plan. On paid plans, you can enable Auto Recharge to automatically purchase more credits when your usage gets close to the limit.

When you reach your plan limit, you will not be able to make any more requests until you upgrade your plan or enable Auto Recharge.

## Advanced endpoints [Permalink for this section](https://supadata.ai/pricing\#advanced-endpoints)

The following endpoints are available on Pro and above:

- `/v1/youtube/transcript/batch`
- `/v1/youtube/transcript/translate`

## Frequently Asked Questions [Permalink for this section](https://supadata.ai/pricing\#frequently-asked-questions)

#### Are changes to my plan applied immediately? [Permalink for this section](https://supadata.ai/pricing\#are-changes-to-my-plan-applied-immediately)

Yes, changes to your plan are applied immediately with proration. This means, that you are charged only a partial amount for the remaining time in the current billing cycle.

#### How do I enable Auto Recharge, change my plan or cancel my subscription? [Permalink for this section](https://supadata.ai/pricing\#how-do-i-enable-auto-recharge-change-my-plan-or-cancel-my-subscription)

You can make changes to your plan in the [dashboard](https://dash.supadata.ai/) Billing section.

#### Do credits roll over to the next month? [Permalink for this section](https://supadata.ai/pricing\#do-credits-roll-over-to-the-next-month)

No, credits do not roll over to the next month. Each month you start with the same amount of credits as your plan.

#### Can I get a refund? [Permalink for this section](https://supadata.ai/pricing\#can-i-get-a-refund)

Yes, monthly plans can be refunded for the last billing period if no API requests were made. Annual plans are non-refundable, except within a 14-day cooldown period after signing up (prorated). Please contact [support@supadata.ai](mailto:support@supadata.ai) to request a refund.
