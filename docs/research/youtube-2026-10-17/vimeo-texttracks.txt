## AI

Use these methods to access Vimeo's AI-powered video intelligence features, including automatic transcription, subtitle translation, audio dubbing, and video Q&A. All endpoints require the \`ai\` OAuth scope and are available exclusively to Enterprise accounts. POST requests are subject to per-endpoint rate limits — contact your account team or Vimeo Sales to increase your allotment. Vimeo AI feature use is subject to \[Vimeo's AI Addendum\](https://vimeo.com/legal/service-terms/ai).
